Four rules that cover most exam questions

  1. Block modes vs stream-like modes. ECB and CBC pad the last block and need DK to decrypt. CFB, OFB and CTR never pad and only ever use EK.
  2. What feeds back? CBC and CFB feed back ciphertext. OFB feeds back the cipher's own output. CTR feeds back nothing, it just counts.
  3. Never reuse an IV or nonce with the same key. In CFB, OFB and CTR a repeat reuses the keystream, so C₁ ⊕ C₂ = P₁ ⊕ P₂ leaks.
  4. Parallel everywhere: CTR, both directions. ECB too, but it is insecure. CBC and CFB decrypt in parallel only. OFB is sequential both ways.

In the exam: draw the block diagram, write the encryption and decryption formulas, then give two advantages and two disadvantages (parallelism, error propagation, padding).
